# Vrinda-Store-Annual-Report-2022
Built an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze annual retail sales and customer behavior. Performed data cleaning and used Pivot Tables, Charts, and Slicers to visualize sales trends, order status, top states, and channel performance, enabling data-driven decision making.

Key Insights from Vrinda Store Dashboard:-

• Women customers contribute the majority of sales (64%), indicating that female shoppers are the primary target segment for the business.

• The Adult age group generates the highest number of orders, showing strong purchasing power in this segment compared to seniors and teenagers.

• A very high delivery success rate (around 92%) reflects efficient order fulfillment, while cancelled, returned, and refunded orders form only a small portion.

• Amazon is the leading sales channel (35%), followed by Myntra and Flipkart, highlighting the importance of marketplace platforms for revenue growth.

• Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top-performing states, contributing the highest revenue and representing key regional markets.

• Sales show a peak in the early months of the year and a gradual decline toward the end, which can help in seasonal planning and marketing strategies.

• Teenagers and seniors contribute less to total orders, suggesting an opportunity for targeted promotions to increase engagement in these segments.



Tools & Techniques Used:-
Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization, Interactive Dashboard.

Business Impact:-
Provides actionable insights to improve sales strategy, customer targeting, and business performance.
